## About Quantum Life University

**Quantum Life University (QLU)** is the vision of New Age Spiritual Master **Dr. Newton Kondaveti, MBBS, MD** and **Dr. Lakshmi Newton, MBBS**.

They are medical doctors who realised that everyone has an “Inner Doctor”, and therefore chose to become **Soul Doctors** instead of practising conventional medicine.

QLU is modelled on the lines of the ancient universities of **Takshashila** and **Nalanda**. Its singular objective is:

> **Awakening Wisdom Within**

The Mission of QLU is to create an open, supportive, unconditional and holistic environment for learning, integrating the teachings, and applying them into one’s daily life, thereby transforming knowledge into profound wisdom.

~ Dr. Newton Kondaveti
